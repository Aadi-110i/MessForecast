How It Works

The Closed-Loop Waste Reduction Engine

From statistical forecast to kitchen preparation and financial auditing, every step reinforces the next.

01

Contextual Forecasting

Adaptive Weighted Moving Average factors in weekday patterns, recent trend momentum, and exam/holiday calendar multipliers.

02

Batch Recipe Scaling

Forecast headcount automatically scales 100-portion standard recipes into exact raw ingredient quantities with configured safety buffers.

03

Offline Attendance PWA

Diners scan in via QR tokens or RFID cards at entry gates. Works uninterrupted during network dropouts and syncs idempotently when reconnected.

04

Waste Cost Accounting

Kitchen logs prepared, served, and leftover portions. Leftovers are weighed and converted into rupee waste cost, feeding accuracy back into future predictions.

Specifications

Technical Architecture & Compliance

Built according to Track P project requirements for campus infrastructure.

Django 5.x Backend

Python-native in-process forecasting engine with modular apps for Menu, Attendance, Calendar, and Analytics.

Relational Database

Normalized relational model linking raw ingredients to batch recipes, attendance scans, and derived waste records.

Offline-First PWA

Service Worker asset caching, client UUID idempotency keys, and offline queueing for uninterrupted gate scanning.

Export & Integrations

One-click CSV indents for campus ERP procurement, Swagger/OpenAPI interactive documentation, and REST APIs.
