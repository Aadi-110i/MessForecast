How the cost is worked out

Portions left is prepared minus served, recorded on the kitchen screen.

Weight is portions left multiplied by the dish’s standard portion size in grams, divided by one thousand.

Cost is portions left multiplied by the dish’s cost per portion, where the cost per portion is the sum of every recipe line: quantity times the current unit cost, including trim loss.

Over-preparation compares the portions prepared against the portions the forecast expected, so the kitchen can tell waste apart from a genuinely busy day.
