The Forecasting Engine

Twelve weeks, and three corrections.

One recency-weighted average of what the hall has actually served, with three corrections laid over it.

The last twelve weeks

Every service at that hall, weighted towards recent weeks so current student habits dominate the baseline.

Recency Weighted

The weekday factor

Fridays and weekends run different turnouts from midweek meals. The specific day pattern is isolated.

Day Calibrated

Weeks an event pulled apart

Exams, cultural fests and holidays are detected and removed before the average is calculated.

Anomaly Filtered

The growth trend

A hall that is steadily growing is never averaged against one that is shrinking. Trend velocity is accounted for.
