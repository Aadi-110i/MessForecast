Attendance is scanned at the door.

Every service is counted as students pass the gate, and the count lands against the hall, the day and the meal. Twelve weeks of those counts is what the forecaster learns from.

Twelve weeks, and three corrections.

One recency-weighted average of what the hall has actually served, with three corrections laid over it.

The last twelve weeks
Every service at that hall, weighted towards the recent ones.
The weekday
Fridays run busier than the rest of the week, so the day is taken out of the average.
Weeks an event pulled apart
Exams and festivals are removed before the average is taken.
The trend
A hall that is growing is not averaged against one that is shrinking.
