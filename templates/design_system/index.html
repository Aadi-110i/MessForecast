The rules that hold everywhere

  • Every control has eight states
    Hover, focus, active, disabled, visited
  • A panel never holds a panel
    One containment layer
  • A list never empties silently
    It says what to do next
  • Labels are sentence case
    Never tracked capitals
  • No emoji as icons
    No middle dots joining metadata
  • Every animation has a reduced-motion fallback
    Only transform and opacity
  • A number is never invented
    It comes from a recorded service
